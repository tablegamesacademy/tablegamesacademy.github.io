Baccarat

Premise

This is a new casino game with three dice based on the traditional game of Cee-Lo. The goal is to have a better qualifying roll than the dealer.

Banker

1 to 1, 10% commission

House Edge: 2.27% | RTP: 97.73%

Shooter

1 to 1

Tie

11 to 1

There's no strategy, Banker and Player have much lower house edge than Tie or any other side bets.
